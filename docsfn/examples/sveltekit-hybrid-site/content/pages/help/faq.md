# FAQ

This route is not in the docs collection. It comes from `content/pages`, which means it can live at `/help/faq` without going through the `/docs` prefix.

## Common questions

### Can I mix landing pages and docs?

Yes. SvelteKit owns the app routes and `docsfn` owns the content pipeline.

### Can I have multiple doc sections?

Yes. Put them in different folders and map them to different sidebar definitions.
